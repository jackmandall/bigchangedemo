So what does BigChange actually do?

Follow one delivery from the phone ringing to the money landing. Every stage below happens in the same system, on the same record.

Assets and PPM
An asset register per site with serial numbers, warranty status and planned maintenance schedules.
Contracts and recurring billing
Set a contract value once and let the periodic invoices generate themselves.
Booking site and payments
A customer portal for bookings and job status, with card, Apple Pay and Google Pay via Stripe.
Alerts and automations
Rule based alerts, custom job flags and follow on jobs raised automatically from a job result.
The Network
Share jobs with subcontractors who are also on BigChange, with status and invoicing flowing back.
Integrations
Xero and Sage 50 sync included, plus a free REST API up to 100,000 calls a month.
Integrated dash cams
Journey video inside the platform, detecting harsh braking, tailgating, phone use and collisions. Hardware and subscription.
Security
Role based permissions, multi factor authentication as standard and a full audit trail.
