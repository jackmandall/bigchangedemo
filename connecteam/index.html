BigChange vs Connecteam, feature by feature

BigChange and Connecteam solve different problems. BigChange is a field service management platform that runs the whole job lifecycle, from scheduling and dispatch through job cards, assets, invoicing and vehicle tracking. Connecteam is a deskless employee management app, strongest on staff scheduling, time tracking, team communication and HR. They overlap on rostering, time, forms and the mobile app; they diverge on field service operations and on employee engagement.

In short

If the job is running field service, scheduling engineers, doing the work on site, invoicing it, and tracking the assets and vehicles behind it, BigChange does it end to end in one platform, and Connecteam does not reach into invoicing, assets, contracts or fleet.

If the job is engaging and administering a deskless workforce, chat, company updates, training, HR, onboarding and time off, Connecteam has more built in, because that is what it is designed for.

For a trade or maintenance business, BigChange covers the whole operation from quote to cash; the employee-engagement tools are where Connecteam adds breadth alongside it.
