So what does BigChange actually do?

Follow one visit from the phone ringing to the money landing. Every stage below happens in the same system, on the same record.

Assets and PPM
An asset register per site with serial numbers, warranty status and planned maintenance schedules.
Contracts and recurring billing
Set a contract value once and let the periodic invoices generate themselves.
Booking site and payments
A customer portal for bookings and job status, with card, Apple Pay and Google Pay via Stripe.
Alerts and automations
Rule based alerts, custom job flags and follow on jobs raised automatically from a job result.
